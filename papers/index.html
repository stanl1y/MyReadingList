力覺 × 接觸豐富操作Force-Aware Contact-Rich Manipulation

這串的共同敵人:力訊號稀疏又吵——大多數時間接觸力≈0,天真地把力丟進 policy 只會讓它更黏視覺、甚至更糟。上位的立場文章(Karcini)主張機器人缺的是把力/具身/獎勵「接地」的機制;底下兩條線各自出招。SJTU · Cewu Lu 線走「控制與架構」:手持力捕捉+混合力位控制(ForceMimic)→ 用接觸預測 gate 掉噪音(FoAR)→ 用 Interaction Frame 拆出力控/位控子空間(Force Policy)。CMU · Pathak 線走「訓練與感測」:視覺模糊 curriculum 逼 policy 先學力(FACTR)→ 讓廉價手臂免感測器也能估力(FACTR 2)。The shared enemy of this bunch: force is sparse and noisy—contact force is ≈0 most of the time, so naively feeding force to a policy makes it lean on vision even more, or worse. The umbrella position paper (Karcini) argues robots lack mechanisms to ground force / embodiment / reward; two lines answer it. The SJTU · Cewu Lu line evolves control & architecture: handheld force capture + hybrid force-position control (ForceMimic) → gating noise via contact prediction (FoAR) → splitting force/motion subspaces with an Interaction Frame (Force Policy). The CMU · Pathak line evolves training & sensing: a vision-blur curriculum that forces the policy to learn force first (FACTR) → sensorless force estimation for commodity arms (FACTR 2).

足式機器人:控制的兩條路Legged Robots: Two Roads to Control

四足控制的兩種世界觀——又一次「模型 vs 學習」的對照(與力覺串同構),而且兩派正在互借工具。Model-based 線:凸 MPC 規劃地面反作用力、500 Hz 全身 QP「追力而非追軌跡」(WBIC,Mini-Cheetah 六種步態、3.7 m/s)→ 把姿態線性化搬到 SO(3) 上、實機穿越 Euler 奇異點後空翻(RF-MPC)→ Wensing 等的 T-RO 綜述畫出整條工具鏈。Learning 線:模仿動物(Motion Imitation)→ 1 秒內適應環境 extrinsics(RMA)→ 一個 policy 多種步態(Walk These Ways)→ 隱式地形想像(DreamWaQ)→ 估「機器人自己的反應」而非環境,實機爬 176.5 階(HIMLoco)→ 把學到的模型接回規劃(PIP-Loco)。行走之外:用腿運球(DribbleBot)、預訓練動作先驗玩鬼抓人(Tencent)、人形的雙層動作模仿(Bi-Level)。Two worldviews for quadruped control — another model-vs-learning contrast (isomorphic to the force thread), now borrowing each other's tools. Model-based line: convex MPC plans ground-reaction forces and a 500 Hz whole-body QP tracks forces, not trajectories (WBIC; six gaits, 3.7 m/s on Mini-Cheetah) → attitude linearization moved onto SO(3), backflipping through the Euler singularity on hardware (RF-MPC) → the Wensing et al. T-RO survey maps the toolchain. Learning line: imitate animals (Motion Imitation) → adapt to extrinsics within a second (RMA) → one policy, many gaits (Walk These Ways) → implicit terrain imagination (DreamWaQ) → estimate the robot's own response instead of the terrain, 176.5 real stairs (HIMLoco) → plug the learned model back into planning (PIP-Loco). Beyond walking: dribbling with legs (DribbleBot), a pre-trained motion prior that plays chase tag (Tencent), bi-level motion imitation for humanoids.

PIP-Loco: A Proprioceptive Infinite Horizon Planning Framework for Quadrupedal Robot Locomotion

Aditya Shirwatkar, Naman Saxena, Kishore Chandra, Shishir Kolathaya

把 MPC 的規劃與約束接回 model-free RL:訓練時以 PPO 非對稱 actor-critic 共同學速度估測器與 Dreamer 模組(觀測空間動力模型、獎勵模型、行為克隆策略、蒸餾自特權 critic 的價值);部署時改由 Dreamer 做 H=10 加學習價值終端成本的「無限視野」受約束 MPPI,同時最佳化動作與速度命令。訓練回報勝 HIMLoco(9.5–9.7 vs 8.6,高雜訊 8.16 vs 1.73),Go1 實機走過斜坡、碎石、樓梯與 45 cm 落差,但規劃本身的效益只在一段 10 秒模擬中定性展示。Plugs MPC-style planning and constraints back into model-free RL: during training a PPO asymmetric actor-critic co-learns a velocity estimator and a Dreamer module (observation-space dynamics, reward model, behavior-cloned policy, value distilled from the privileged critic); at deployment the Dreamer runs an H=10 constrained MPPI with a learned terminal value (infinite-horizon in effect), optimizing both actions and the velocity command.
