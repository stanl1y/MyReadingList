力覺 × 接觸豐富操作Force-Aware Contact-Rich Manipulation

這串的共同敵人:力訊號稀疏又吵——大多數時間接觸力≈0,天真地把力丟進 policy 只會讓它更黏視覺、甚至更糟。上位的立場文章(Karcini)主張機器人缺的是把力/具身/獎勵「接地」的機制;底下兩條線各自出招。SJTU · Cewu Lu 線走「控制與架構」:手持力捕捉+混合力位控制(ForceMimic)→ 用接觸預測 gate 掉噪音(FoAR)→ 用 Interaction Frame 拆出力控/位控子空間(Force Policy)。CMU · Pathak 線走「訓練與感測」:視覺模糊 curriculum 逼 policy 先學力(FACTR)→ 讓廉價手臂免感測器也能估力(FACTR 2)。The shared enemy of this bunch: force is sparse and noisy—contact force is ≈0 most of the time, so naively feeding force to a policy makes it lean on vision even more, or worse. The umbrella position paper (Karcini) argues robots lack mechanisms to ground force / embodiment / reward; two lines answer it. The SJTU · Cewu Lu line evolves control & architecture: handheld force capture + hybrid force-position control (ForceMimic) → gating noise via contact prediction (FoAR) → splitting force/motion subspaces with an Interaction Frame (Force Policy). The CMU · Pathak line evolves training & sensing: a vision-blur curriculum that forces the policy to learn force first (FACTR) → sensorless force estimation for commodity arms (FACTR 2).

足式機器人:控制的兩條路Legged Robots: Two Roads to Control

四足控制的兩種世界觀——又一次「模型 vs 學習」的對照(與力覺串同構)。Model-based 線:把動力學寫成模型、線上解最佳化——凸 MPC 規劃地面反作用力、500 Hz 全身 QP「追力而非追軌跡」(WBIC),Mini-Cheetah 用同一組參數跑六種步態、最高 3.7 m/s;Wensing 等的 T-RO 綜述把「接觸建模→簡化模型→數值轉錄→全身 QP」整條工具鏈畫成地圖。Learning 線:模擬器大規模 RL、零樣本上真機——模仿動物(Motion Imitation)→ 1 秒內線上適應環境 extrinsics(RMA)→ 一個 policy 多種步態的行為旋鈕(Walk These Ways)→ 非對稱 actor-critic 的隱式地形想像、單卡一小時訓完(DreamWaQ)。跨串彩蛋:RMA 出自 Pathak 組——與力覺串的 FACTR 同一實驗室。Two worldviews for quadruped control — another model-vs-learning contrast (isomorphic to the force thread). Model-based line: write down the dynamics and optimize online — convex MPC plans ground-reaction forces, a 500 Hz whole-body QP tracks forces rather than trajectories (WBIC); Mini-Cheetah runs six gaits up to 3.7 m/s with one parameter set; the Wensing et al. T-RO survey maps the whole toolchain (contact modeling → reduced models → transcription → whole-body QP). Learning line: massive RL in sim, zero-shot to hardware — imitate animals (Motion Imitation) → adapt online to environment extrinsics within a second (RMA) → one policy, many gaits via behavior knobs (Walk These Ways) → implicit terrain imagination with asymmetric actor-critic, trained in one hour on one GPU (DreamWaQ). Cross-thread egg: RMA comes from the Pathak lab — the same lab as FACTR in the force thread.

Segment Anything 系列The Segment Anything Series

主線是把「分割」做成可提示的 foundation model,然後一代一代擴張「可提示 / 可輸出」的維度——SAM 用空間提示(點/框/遮罩)在單張影像分割任意物體(靠 SA-1B 資料引擎);SAM 2 加時間(streaming memory 推廣到影片);SAM 3 加語意(文字概念/範例提示,一次找出並追蹤某概念的所有實例 PCS);SAM 3D 加維度(單張影像生成完整 per-object 3D:形狀+紋理+layout)。共同配方:每一代都配一個 model-in-the-loop 資料引擎,讓模型自產大部分標註。SAM 3D 與下面「3D 視覺」串的 VGGT 是姊妹——前者生成完整物體 3D,後者回復可視場景幾何。The throughline: make segmentation a promptable foundation model, then widen the promptable / output space generation by generation—SAM segments any object in one image via spatial prompts (points/boxes/masks), powered by the SA-1B data engine; SAM 2 adds time (streaming memory extends it to video); SAM 3 adds semantics (text-concept / exemplar prompts that find & track every instance of a concept — PCS); SAM 3D adds dimension (full per-object 3D — shape+texture+layout — from one image). Shared recipe: every generation pairs with a model-in-the-loop data engine so the model annotates most of its own data. SAM 3D is a sibling to VGGT in the 3D-vision thread below — the former generates full object 3D, the latter recovers visible scene geometry.

RL:為控制而生的表徵RL: Representations for Control

同一個底層問題——用 TD 學一個「為控制而生」的 latent 表徵——兩篇下了相反的注。FB(2021)走 task-free:表徵一次編碼「所有 reward 的最優策略」,事後零規劃直接讀出;TD-MPC(2022)走 task-oriented:latent 只為單一任務的 reward/value 服務(TOLD),配上 MPPI 規劃,首次解掉 DMControl Dog(38 維動作)。通用性 vs 單任務效率——這對「對照組」圈出控制表徵光譜的兩端。One underlying question — learn a control-born latent via TD — and two opposite bets. FB (2021) goes task-free: one representation encodes optimal policies for every reward, read out later with zero planning. TD-MPC (2022) goes task-oriented: the latent serves a single task (TOLD) and pairs with MPPI planning, first to solve DMControl Dog (38-D actions). Generality vs single-task efficiency — a contrast pair spanning the spectrum of control representations.

部署後的 LLM 是靜態的:重訓太貴、微調會災難性遺忘、ICL 出了 context 就忘。這篇提出仿腦的 Wake/Sleep 範式:醒著吸收,睡著鞏固——用「向上蒸餾」把知識種進更大的自己(新長出的 low-rank MoE expert),再加 Dreaming(自生成資料的自我改進);並把災難性遺忘重新框成容量問題而非取樣問題。Deployed LLMs are static: retraining is costly, fine-tuning forgets catastrophically, ICL forgets outside the context. This work proposes a brain-inspired Wake/Sleep paradigm: absorb while awake, consolidate while asleep — seeding knowledge into a strictly larger self (a newly grown low-rank MoE expert) via upward distillation — plus Dreaming (self-generated self-improvement), reframing catastrophic forgetting as a capacity problem rather than a sampling one.

Language Models Need Sleep: Learning to Self-Modify and Consolidate Memories

Ali Behrouz, Farnoosh Hashemi, Adel Javanmard, Vahab Mirrokni

把人類「睡眠鞏固記憶」搬進 LLM:清醒時吸收新知,睡眠時用向上蒸餾(Knowledge Seeding)把短期記憶固化進新長出的長期參數,再用 Dreaming 自我改進,對抗災難性遺忘。Brings human sleep-based memory consolidation to LLMs: a wake phase absorbs data, a sleep phase uses upward distillation (Knowledge Seeding) to fix short-term memories into newly grown long-term parameters, then Dreaming self-improves, fighting catastrophic forgetting.
