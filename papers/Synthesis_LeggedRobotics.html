One Reading List, Four Territories

TL;DR

singhaman1750/Legged-Robots 是一份足式機器人的研究書單,約 150 條,橫跨四塊領土:控制(模型派 vs 學習派)、機器人本體設計(人形與四足平台)、致動器與傳動(行星、擺線、SEA、雙馬達)、協同設計(硬體與控制一起最佳化)。這頁把每一條都放進可搜尋的雙語表格,附一句話定位與取得方式;其中 篇在本站有完整六分頁導讀(📖)。讀這份書單最重要的一件事:硬體決定了控制能用什麼假設,控制又反過來決定硬體該怎麼設計——四塊領土其實是一個迴圈。

singhaman1750/Legged-Robots is a legged-robotics reading list of about 150 entries across four territories: control (model-based vs learning), robot design (humanoid and quadruped platforms), actuators and transmissions (planetary, cycloidal, SEA, dual-motor) and co-design (optimizing hardware and control together). This page puts every entry into a searchable bilingual table with a one-line positioning and how to get it; of them have a full six-tab guide in this library (📖). The one idea to carry through the list: hardware fixes which assumptions control can make, and control in turn dictates how the hardware should be designed — the four territories form a loop.

SECTION 02

Control: Two Worldviews, Converging

足式控制的核心難題:接觸是離散的(腳要嘛著地要嘛沒有)、動力學高度非線性、決策要在毫秒級完成。書單的 17 篇分成兩條主線。模型派把機器人簡化成單剛體,線上解 MPC 規劃地面反作用力,再交給全身 QP 追蹤(Convex MPC → WBIC → RF-MPC);它假設簡化模型夠準、接觸時序可事先排好。學習派在模擬器裡大規模 RL,零樣本轉到真機(RMA → Walk These Ways → DreamWaQ → HIMLoco);它假設模擬 + 隨機化涵蓋了真實世界,並把「看不到的環境」交給從本體感覺歷史估測的 latent。

The core difficulty of legged control: contact is discrete (a foot is either down or not), the dynamics are highly nonlinear, and decisions must be made within milliseconds. The list's 17 control papers split into two main lines. Model-based reduces the robot to a single rigid body, solves an MPC online to plan ground-reaction forces, and hands them to a whole-body QP for tracking (Convex MPC → WBIC → RF-MPC); it assumes the reduced model is accurate and the contact schedule can be fixed in advance. Learning-based runs massive RL in simulation and transfers zero-shot (RMA → Walk These Ways → DreamWaQ → HIMLoco); it assumes simulation plus randomization covers reality and hands the unseen environment to a latent estimated from proprioceptive history.

兩派正在互相借東西:Feedback MPC 把 MPC 的回授策略直接放進力矩層、PIP-Loco 把 RL 學到的模型與價值函數接回線上規劃;學習派也從「估環境參數」(RMA)走向「估機器人自己的反應」(HIMLoco)。書單的人形控制只有一篇(Bi-Level Imitation,且只在模擬),是最明顯的缺口。導讀補充

The two camps are borrowing from each other: Feedback MPC puts the MPC's feedback policy straight into the torque loop, and PIP-Loco plugs RL-learned models and value functions back into online planning; the learning line itself moved from estimating environment parameters (RMA) to estimating the robot's own response (HIMLoco). Humanoid control has just one entry on the list (Bi-Level Imitation, simulation only) — the clearest gap.guide note

SECTION 03

Robot Design: Platforms Through the Years

人形(22 台)大致有三個世代 導讀補充:2009–2019 是大型研究/救災人形(LOLA、COMAN、TORO、JAXON、WALK-MAN、DRC-HUBO+、HRP-5P),重點是功率、剛性與 DRC 等級的任務;2021–2023 轉向動態與本體感知致動器(Tello、MIT Humanoid);2024–2025 爆發一波開源、低成本、「為學習而設計」的平台(Berkeley Humanoid、Berkeley Humanoid Lite、ToddlerBot、AGILOped、PANDORA、ARTEMIS)。

Humanoids (22) fall roughly into three generations guide note: 2009–2019 large research and disaster-response humanoids (LOLA, COMAN, TORO, JAXON, WALK-MAN, DRC-HUBO+, HRP-5P), focused on power, stiffness and DRC-class tasks; 2021–2023 a turn to dynamic motion and proprioceptive actuators (Tello, MIT Humanoid); 2024–2025 a burst of open-source, low-cost, learning-ready platforms (Berkeley Humanoid, Berkeley Humanoid Lite, ToddlerBot, AGILOped, PANDORA, ARTEMIS).

四足(26 篇)書單按驅動方式與重量分表:液壓(HyQ 系列,45–91 kg)→ 電動小型(Minitaur、Stanford Doggo、Solo、Stoch,2–9 kg)→ Mini Cheetah 級(9–13 kg)→ 中型(StarlETH/ANYmal 的 SEA 路線、MIT Cheetah 的本體感知路線)→ 大型高負載(Cheetah 3、Tachyon、HOUND、Kirin、Barry、RAIBO2)。讀這區時,把每台都問一句:它的腿用什麼傳動、減速比多少?答案通常解釋了它的控制方式(見 04 ACTUATORS)。

Quadrupeds (26) are tabled by actuation and mass: hydraulic (the HyQ line, 45–91 kg) → small electric (Minitaur, Stanford Doggo, Solo, Stoch, 2–9 kg) → Mini Cheetah class (9–13 kg) → mid-size (the SEA route of StarlETH/ANYmal, the proprioceptive route of MIT Cheetah) → large high-payload (Cheetah 3, Tachyon, HOUND, Kirin, Barry, RAIBO2). For each robot ask one question: what transmission drives the legs, and at what gear ratio? The answer usually explains its control approach (see 04 ACTUATORS).

SECTION 04

Actuators: The Gear-Ratio Trade-off

致動器區的 40 多條,幾乎都在回答同一個取捨 導讀補充:馬達本身力矩小、轉速高,所以要減速。減速比 \(N\) 放大輸出力矩,但也把馬達轉子慣量以 \(N^2\) 放大到關節端:

The 40-odd actuator entries nearly all answer one trade-off guide note: a motor has low torque and high speed, so it needs a reduction. A gear ratio \(N\) amplifies output torque, but it also amplifies the rotor inertia seen at the joint by \(N^2\):

$$\tau_{\text{out}} = \eta\, N\, K_t\, i, \qquad J_{\text{refl}} = N^2 J_{\text{rotor}}, \qquad P_{\text{Joule}} = i^2 R = \frac{\tau_m^2}{K_m^2},\quad K_m=\frac{K_t}{\sqrt{R}}$$

高減速比 = 力矩大但難以反驅、撞擊時傷齒輪、力只能靠額外感測器量;低減速比的準直驅(QDD,約 6:1 單級行星,Mini Cheetah 路線)讓馬達電流本身就是力矩感測器(\(\tau \approx N K_t i\)),這正是凸 MPC 與 sim-to-real RL 都仰賴的「透明」硬體。其餘路線各解決 QDD 的一個弱點:擺線單級就有高減速比、可 3D 列印,但要處理背隙;SEA 用彈簧吸收衝擊、以形變量力;雙馬達/變速讓一顆致動器有「高速」與「大力矩」兩個工作點;致動器建模則把力矩-轉速限制帶進控制器與 RL。

A high ratio means big torque but poor backdrivability, gear damage on impact, and force that needs extra sensors; a low-ratio quasi-direct drive (QDD, about 6:1 single-stage planetary, the Mini Cheetah route) turns motor current itself into a torque sensor (\(\tau \approx N K_t i\)) — the transparent hardware both convex MPC and sim-to-real RL rely on. The other routes each patch one QDD weakness: cycloidal gets a high single-stage ratio and is 3D-printable but must fight backlash; SEA absorbs impacts with a spring and measures force by its deflection; dual-motor / variable transmissions give one actuator both a high-speed and a high-torque operating point; actuator modeling brings torque-speed limits into controllers and RL.

SECTION 05

Co-Design: Optimizing the Body and the Brain Together

協同設計是書單最大、也最新的一區(45 篇,超過一半是 2024 年以後)。共同的數學形狀是一個巢狀最佳化 導讀補充:

Co-design is the list's largest and newest section (45 entries, more than half from 2024 on). They share one mathematical shape, a nested optimization guide note:

$$d^\star = \arg\max_{d \in \mathcal{D}} \; J\big(d,\ \pi^\star(d)\big), \qquad \pi^\star(d) = \arg\max_{\pi} J(d, \pi)$$

難處在內層:每評估一個設計 \(d\),都要重新求一次控制器 \(\pi^\star(d)\),非常昂貴。書單的分類其實就是「怎麼攤提這個成本」的不同答案:模型派用軌跡最佳化/最佳控制求內層,外層用梯度、ADMM 或隨機規劃(Dinev 2022、Robust Co-Design、Vitruvio);RL 派讓一個「知道設計」的策略同時適用許多設計(Meta-RL design、PEA co-design、Morphological pretraining),或乾脆把「改設計」變成策略的動作(Transform2Act、Hardware as Policy);2025–2026 則出現代理模型、演化 + RL、LLM/Transformer 生成設計。

The hard part is the inner loop: every candidate design \(d\) needs its own controller \(\pi^\star(d)\), which is expensive. The list's categories are really different answers to how to amortize that cost: model-based work solves the inner loop with trajectory optimization / optimal control and the outer loop with gradients, ADMM or stochastic programming (Dinev 2022, Robust Co-Design, Vitruvio); RL work trains one design-aware policy that serves many designs (Meta-RL design, PEA co-design, morphological pretraining), or turns changing the design into a policy action (Transform2Act, Hardware as Policy); 2025–2026 adds surrogates, evolution plus RL, and LLM/Transformer design generators.

最大的缺口:多數工作只在模擬中驗證,真的把最佳化出來的設計做成硬體、再證明它更好的論文很少。

The biggest gap: most work is validated only in simulation; papers that actually build the optimized design and show it is better on hardware are rare.

SECTION 06

Roadmap: Four Ways Through the List

路線 A · 我想做 RL 運動控制

Route A · I want to do RL locomotion

  1. Motion Imitation sim-to-real RL 的基本配方:參考動作 + 動態隨機化 + 真機適應。The basic sim-to-real RL recipe: reference motion + dynamics randomization + on-robot adaptation.
  2. RMA → DreamWaQ → HIMLoco 「看不到的環境」怎麼估:特權參數蒸餾 → 非對稱 critic + VAE → 估機器人自己的反應。How to estimate what you cannot see: privileged-parameter distillation → asymmetric critic + VAE → estimating the robot's own response.
  3. Walk These Ways → DribbleBot 同一實驗室:從「一條策略多種步態」到「用腿操作物體」。Same lab: from one policy with many gaits to manipulating objects with the legs.
  4. Tencent Lifelike · Bi-Level Imitation 動作先驗的兩種延伸:預訓練可重用的生成式先驗;讓參考動作本身也被最佳化。Two extensions of motion priors: a reusable pre-trained generative prior; letting the reference itself be optimized.
  5. Actuator-Constrained RL · Berkeley Humanoid 回頭看硬體:把致動器限制放進 RL,或乾脆把硬體設計成好模擬的樣子。Back to hardware: put actuator limits into RL, or design the hardware so it is easy to simulate.

路線 B · 我想懂模型式控制

Route B · I want to understand model-based control

  1. Wensing 綜述 先拿到整張地圖:接觸建模、簡化模型、轉錄、全身 QP。Get the whole map first: contact modeling, reduced models, transcription, whole-body QP.
  2. Convex MPC (Cheetah 3) 凸 MPC 的原點;本站因 DSpace 限制抓取尚未導讀,可在 02 CONTROL 表格點 FREE ↗ 讀全文。The origin of convex MPC; not yet guided here because DSpace blocks scripted downloads — read it via the FREE ↗ link in the 02 CONTROL table.
  3. WBIC + MPC → RF-MPC 加上全身脈衝控制;再把姿態線性化搬到 SO(3) 上,做大角度動作。Add whole-body impulse control; then move attitude linearization onto SO(3) for large-rotation motions.
  4. PIP-Loco 兩派的交會:把 RL 學到的東西接回規劃器。Where the camps meet: plugging what RL learned back into a planner.

路線 C · 我想自己做一台機器人

Route C · I want to build a robot

  1. MIT Cheetah design principles (2013) 本體感知致動器的設計哲學從這裡開始(03 ROBOT DESIGN 表格有 FREE ↗ 期刊版)。Where the proprioceptive-actuator philosophy begins (03 ROBOT DESIGN table has the FREE ↗ journal version).
  2. Stanford Doggo 用現成無人機馬達 + 皮帶做 QDD,看一台低成本四足怎麼權衡。QDD from off-the-shelf drone motors and a belt: how a low-cost quadruped makes its trade-offs.
  3. Berkeley Humanoid Lite 3D 列印擺線致動器 + 測試方法 + 整台人形 BOM。3D-printed cycloidal actuators, the test methodology and a whole-humanoid BOM.
  4. 04 ACTUATORS 的行星 / 擺線 / 雙馬達表格 + 測功機資源The planetary / cycloidal / dual-motor tables in 04 ACTUATORS, plus the dyno resources 按你要的減速比與功率挑路線。Pick a route by the ratio and power you need.

路線 D · 我想做協同設計

Route D · I want to do co-design

  1. Embodied Co-Design 綜述 先看分類法,知道自己的問題落在哪一格。Start with the taxonomy to see which cell your problem falls in.
  2. Versatile Co-Design 模型派:外層設計 × 內層最佳控制。Model-based: outer design loop × inner optimal control.

CROSS-CUTTING

1. 透明硬體是兩派控制的共同前提。QDD 讓電流 ≈ 力矩,MPC 才能直接下力指令、模擬器裡的致動器模型才夠準。

2. 致動器模型正在回到學習裡。從 RL 忽略馬達極限,到把力矩-轉速限制寫進訓練(HOUND),再到為了好模擬而設計硬體(Berkeley Humanoid)。

3. 開源與 3D 列印是近五年的主旋律。Doggo、Solo、PADWQ、BH Lite、ToddlerBot、AGILOped、PANDORA、ARTEMIS。

4. 協同設計從模型派移向學習派,但硬體驗證仍是瓶頸。導讀補充

1. Transparent hardware is a shared premise of both control camps. QDD makes current ≈ torque, so MPC can command forces directly and simulated actuator models stay accurate.

2. Actuator models are coming back into learning. From RL ignoring motor limits, to writing torque-speed limits into training (HOUND), to designing hardware that is easy to simulate (Berkeley Humanoid).

3. Open source and 3D printing dominate the last five years. Doggo, Solo, PADWQ, BH Lite, ToddlerBot, AGILOped, PANDORA, ARTEMIS.

4. Co-design is moving from model-based to learning-based, but hardware validation remains the bottleneck.guide note
